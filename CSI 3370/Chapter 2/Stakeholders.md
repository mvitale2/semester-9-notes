A stakeholder is a person who have an interest in the successful implementation of the system.

## Types of stakeholders
- Internal 
	- Persons within the organization that interact with the system
- External 
	- Persons who are interested in the system's operation or success
- Operational 
	- Persons who regularly interact with the system
- Executive
	- Persons who don't directly use the system but manage the system
- Client
	- An entity providing money to develop the system

Stakeholders can be of one or multiple types. 

[[Information Gathering Techniques|Identifying the expectations of your stakeholders]] can be incredibly valuable when collecting feedback.
