## Interviewing
- Prepare detailed questions
- Meet with individuals or groups
- Discuss answers to questions
### Possible questions
- What are the business operations and processes?
- How should those operations be performed?
- What information is needed to perform those operations?
### Preparation
Before
- Establish objective
- Determine correct participants
- Determine questions
- Review documentation
- Set time & location
- Inform participants of details
After
- Look for exception and error conditions
- Gather details
- Use obsidian to write notes
- Document unanswered questions
