- How do we define [[Requirements]]? After collecting information, create models.
- A model is a representation of some aspect of the system being built.
- Types of models
	- Textual
		- Something written
	- Graphical
		- Diagram, schematic
	- Mathematical
		- Formulas, statistics, algorithms
- Unified Modeling Language
	- A standard graphical language
	- Types of UML models
		- Use case, class, location, sequence, communication, state machine
## Reasons for Modeling
- Reducing complexity by abstraction
- Record details for future maintenance/enhancement
- Helps with communication
## Documenting Workflows with Activity Diagrams
A **workflow** is a sequence of processing steps that completely handles one business transaction or customer request. 
### Symbols
- Swimlane heading (horizontal rule)
- Pseudo activity (start & end, bullet)
- Transition arrow
- Activity (oval with text)
- Synchronization Bar (split & join, a horizontal rule with two arrows splitting from it)
- Decision activity (rhombus)
