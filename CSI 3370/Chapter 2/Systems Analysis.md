This area has a whole field of work dedicated to it. It's called software engineering, but some of them are called systems analysts. This is why my major is information technology. 

These five activities comprise systems analysis:
- [Gather detailed information]()
	- interviews questionnaires, documents, etc.
- Define [[Requirements]]
	- modeling functional requirements and non-functional requirements
- Prioritize [[Requirements]]
	- essential, important, vs nice to have features
- [Develop user-interface dialogs](Mödeling)
	- flow of interaction between user and system
- Evaluate [[Requirements]] with users
	- [[Information Gathering Techniques|User involvement and feedback]]

Projects go through at least 6 iterations, where as the iterations become greater, the design process becomes less important and the system components and tests (coding) become more important.
