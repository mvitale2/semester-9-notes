All [[Assets]] of an organization must be classified according to their importance and according to the impact of security breaches affecting the organization. 

An organization should ensure the link between a given type of information and its classification remains consistent. 

[[Best Practices|ISO]] 27001, *ISMS Requirements*, declares the following requirements for information classification:
- Classification of information
- Labeling of Information
- Handling of assets
## Identifying information types
- Electronic data
- Electric communication (email, text, etc.)
- Spoken communication
- Multimedia information (video presentations, surveillance camera recordings)
- Physical information (paper)

SP 800-60 suggests three general organizational areas where information types are defined:
- Mission-based
	- Info that relates specifically to the organization's objectives
- Services delivery support functions
	- Info that relates to the primary services of the organization
- Back office support functions
	- Activities that enable the organization operate effectively
## Organizational Input
To ensure categorization is consistent, these inputs can be critical:
- [[Law|Laws]], directives, and [[policy]]
- [[Information Security|Strategic goals and objectives]]
- Resource availability
- Supply chain
