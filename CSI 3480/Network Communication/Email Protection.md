NIST provides SP-800-177, which provides guidelines for enhancing trust in email.
# Acceptable Use Policy For Email
Employees should only use email for uses that supports the goals and objectives of the organization. Everything else is banned.

The organization has the right to:
- Monitor
	- Examine and inspect any data in email systems
- Sanction
	- Enact disciplinary action on employees who have violated [[Policy]].
- Agreement
	- The company can require employees to sign an agreement to use email. 
# Security [[Controls]]
- Protect messages from unauthorized access, modification, or DDoS
- Emails should have correct addressing and transportation
- Obtain electronic signatures
- Obtaining prior approval for using public services like instant messaging, social networking, or file sharing
- Strong levels of access control
