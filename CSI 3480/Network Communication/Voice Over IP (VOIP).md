# [[Threats]]
- An attacker could listen in on unencrypted VoIP traffic.
- Older [[Firewalls]] may not recognize VoIP traffic.
- Older VoIP phones may require patches.
- Out-of-the-box devices may have default passwords and open ports.
- DoS attacks can be launched against a VoIP phone system.
