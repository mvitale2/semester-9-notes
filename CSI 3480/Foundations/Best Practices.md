[[law|Laws]] and [[policy|policies]] only deter people if the following is true: 
- Fear of penalty 
- Probability of being apprehended 
- Probability of penalty being applied 

Organizations that publish documents about best security practices: 
- NIST (National Insitute of Standards and Technology) 
- ITU-T (International Telecommunications Unit) 
- ISO (International Organization for Standardization) 
- Internet Society 
- PCI (Payment Card Industry) 
- ISACA 
- Center for Internet Security

**ISO/IEC 27000 Suite of Information Security Standards** is one of the most important security standards.
