Things such as data, hardware, software, facilities, or documentation.

Assets can tangible or intangible; things such as computers, reputation, databases, equipment, and personnel. [[Threats]] reduce the value of these assets.

**Business assets** are human resources, business processes, or buildings.

An **asset register** is critical for an organization to properly document their assets. Usually this register will have some form identification for each object. For example, every computer or laptop has a serial number.
