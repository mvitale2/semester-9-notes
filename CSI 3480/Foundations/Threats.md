# Sources
Threat sources are categorized into three areas:
- Environmental
	- floods. earthquakes, tornadoes, power failure
- Business resources
	- equipment failure, supply chain disruption,  unintentional harm
- Hostile actors
	- hackers, insider threats, criminals, nation-state actors
- Structural
	- Failures of equipment, software, resource depletion, etc.
- Accidental
	- Erroneous actions taken by individuals
# STRIDE
A threat classification system developed by Microsoft.
### Spoofing identity
Illegally accessing and then using another user's authentication information. This violates [[Information Security|confidentiality]]. 
[[Controls]] that can be used are in the area of authentication. 
### Tampering with data
Involves the modification of data. 
Relevant [[controls]] are in the area of integrity (such as encryption). 
### Repudiation
Users who deny performing an action without other parties having any way to prove otherwise. 
Relevant [[controls]] are in the area of non-repudiation, such as logging. 
### Information disclosure
Exposure of information to those who shouldn't have access.
Relevant [[controls]] are in the area of [[Information Security|confidentiality]].
### Denial of service
[[Attack|Attacks]] that deny service to valid users.
Relevant security [[controls]] are in the area of availability (such as firewalls). 
### Elevation of privilege
When an [[Attack|attacker]] has become trusted by the system itself.
Relevant security [[controls]] are in the area of authorization.
