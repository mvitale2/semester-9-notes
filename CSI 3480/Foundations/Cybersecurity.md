The collection of best practices used to protect a [[cyberspace]] environment, organization, and users' [[assets]].

There are three layers in cybersecurity: products, people, and policies. 
# Management Process
An unending process where you constantly analyze the organization's current situation against the standards, [[best practices]], and IT ecosystem:

- Plan & Select controls – assess the risk and address the risk 
- Implement & Deploy – selection for controls, allocation of resources, implementation of controls 
- Audit & Measure
- Update & Improve

The end result is to protect the [[privacy]] of the end user and employees.
