The steps that an [[attack|attacker]] needs to take in order to carry out an attack.

1. Reconnaissance - gathering information
2. Weaponization - the medium the attacker uses
3. Delivery - how the payload is delivered
4. Exploit - The payload exploits a [[Vulnerabilities|vulnerability]] 
5. Installation - The [[malware]] is installed on the [[Assets|asset]]
6. Command and control - The attacker operates the [[malware]] remotely
7. Actions - The attacker uses the [[malware]] to achieve their goals
