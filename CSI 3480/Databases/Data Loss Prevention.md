Preventing sensitive data from being leaked or destroyed. 

Data states:
- At rest
	-  Locate and catalog sensitive information throughout the enterprise.
- Processing
	- Monitor and control the movement of sensitive information across enterprise networks.
- Transmitting
	- Monitor and control the movement of sensitive information on end-user systems.
# Digital Rights Management
Systems and procedures which ensure that holders of digital rights are clearly identified and receive the stipulated payment for their works. 

DRM should meet the following objectives:
- Provide persistent content protection
- Support a variety of digital content types and platforms
- Facilitate content distribution
## [[Best Practices]]
- Create a document classification matrix prior to implementing the solution
- Strive to make DRM user-friendly
- Review logs and alerts regularly and address suspicious events
- Regularly carry out gap analysis that details the differential between the organization's current risk level for data loss
