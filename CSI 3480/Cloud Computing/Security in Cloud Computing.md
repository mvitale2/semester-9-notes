NIST Special Publication 800-144, **Guidelines on Security and Privacy in Public Cloud Computing**

An organization must consider these issues when moving data to the cloud:
- Confidentiality and privacy
	- The consumers' data is not shared with any other third-party
- Data breach responsibilities
	- Notifying users of breaches
	- Risks to intellectual properties
- E-discovery
- [[Risk]] assessment
- Business continuity
- Legal issues
# [[Threats]]
- Responsibility ambiguity
- Loss of governance
- Loss of trust
- Service provider lock-in
	- When an organization transfers data to a service, but can't move that data to another service
- Non secure cloud service user access
- Lack of asset management
- Data loss and leakage
# Service Agreement
The Cloud Standards Customer Councils lists these as major components of a Cloud Service Agreement:
- Customer agreement
	- Describes the overall relationship between the provider and the customer.
- Acceptable use
- Cloud service level agreements
- [[Privacy]] [[Policy|policies]]
