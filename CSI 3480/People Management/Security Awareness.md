A [[security]] awareness program should reach all employees, not just those with access to IT resources. 

All employees should have suitable awareness training based on the level of their responsibilities. 

To develop such a program, ENISA (European Union Agency for Network and Information Security) has identified three main steps:
- Plan, assess, & design
	- Establish initial program team
	- Define target group
	- Develop the program and checklist of task
- Execute & Manage
- Evaluate & Adjust

Example metrics to assess success:
- [[Security]] incidents due to human behavior
- Audit findings
- Results of staff surveys
- Tests of whether staff follow correct procedures
- Number of staff completing training
