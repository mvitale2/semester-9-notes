All aspects of [[security]] related to the behavior of employees and others who have access to the organization's information and systems. 

This area covers all [[security]]-related aspects concerning employees.
1. Hiring new employees
2. Training employees
3. Monitoring employee behavior
4. Handling employee departure/termination

Educating employees on the company's [[Policy|policies]], [[Best Practices]], and current [[Threats]] is an extremely important aspect.

Ensuring the proper [[Controls]] are in place to enable remote work. 

Properly offboarding employees is imperative to ensure [[Information Security]]. 
## Principles for Personnel Security
- Least privilege
	- Give as limited access as possible to employees.
	- Restricted access to [[Assets]] such as accounts, networks, programs, computers, backups, peripherals.
- Separation of duties
	- People involved with monitoring others should not be able to perpetrate inappropriate use.
- Limited reliance on key employees
	- There should be plans in place in case important employees become compromised
- Dual Operator Policy
- Mandatory vacations
