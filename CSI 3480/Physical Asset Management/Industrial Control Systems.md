Hardware that controls industrial processes, such as manufacturing, product handling, production, and distribution. 

ICS is operating in real-time. Rebooting machines, for example, extends downtime and limits production. 

IT systems manage data, while ICS manage physical objects. 

Software changes to ICS must be considered carefully because one change could cause problems for the rest of the system. 

Control systems have limited memory and storage compared to standard PC's. This makes it more difficult to implement software security. ICS also use many older or proprietary operating systems. 
## [[Threats]]
- Blocked or delayed flow of information
- Unauthorized changes
- Inaccurate information
- Interference with equipment operation
- Interference with safety systems operation
